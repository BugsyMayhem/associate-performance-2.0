      Metric Definitions & Calculation Formulas

      Comprehensive guide to key performance indicators across OPD Fulfillment

      Active Pick Speed (IPH) Active Time

      Measures item picking velocity exclusively while an associate is actively logged into a pick walk.

      Formula: (Total Picked Items) ÷ (Active Pick Hours)
      True Shift PPH Shift Time

      Measures true labor efficiency across total scheduled shift hours (including setup, dwell, and secondary tasks).

      Formula: (Total Picked Items) ÷ (Scheduled Shift Hours)
      Picker Utilization % Ratio

      Percentage of total scheduled shift hours spent actively picking on pick walks.

      Formula: (Active Pick Hours ÷ Scheduled Shift Hours) × 100
      FTPR (First Time Pick Rate) Accuracy

      Percentage of requested items located and scanned on the first attempt without substitutions or nil-picks.

      Formula: (FTP Actual Items ÷ FTP Expected Items) × 100
      Non-Pick Shift Hours Time

      Shift hours spent performing secondary roles (dispense, staging, quality checks, or downtime).

      Formula: Max(0, Scheduled Shift Hours − Active Pick Hours)
      Substitutions & Nil Pick Ratio Exceptions

      Proportion of requested items that required item substitutions or resulted in out-of-stock nil-picks.

      Formula: (Substitutions or Nil Picks ÷ Total Picked) × 100
